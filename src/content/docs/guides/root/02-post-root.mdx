---
title: Post-Root Suggestions
description: Tips and suggestions on what to do with your headset after it is rooted.
---

Once you have rooted the headset, the following steps are recommended to improve your experience.

- Install [Pico-4/Settings](https://github.com/Pico-4/Settings/releases/download/0.1/settings.apk), to gain access
  to stock Android settings. Look for the yellow tinted settings icon.

- If any root tools you want to use require [LSPosed](https://github.com/LSPosed/LSPosed/releases/latest),
  use the official build and use
  [LSPosed2Launcher](https://github.com/fzer0x/LSPosed2Launcher/releases/download/1-1.0/LSPosed.Launcher.SU.apk)
  to easily get into the LSPosed manager.
