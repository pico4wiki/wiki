## Installing Magisk
<Steps>
1. You will need a copy of ADB. If it is not installed on your computer, it is included with PicoUnlock.
2. Download a [stock OTA image](/guides/ota) for your firmware version, and extract the `boot.img` to your computer.
3. Download Magisk4Pico from the [downloads page](/downloads) (stock Magisk will NOT work!).
4. `adb push boot.img /sdcard/Download`
5. `adb install Magisk4Pico.apk`
6. Open the newly installed Magisk app on your headset, `Install > Select and Patch a File`, and select `boot.img`.
7. In the output log, you will see the path to the freshly-patched boot image.
8. `adb pull <path_to_patched_boot_image_on_headset>`
9. `adb reboot bootloader`
10. `fastboot oem picoXXXXXXXX unlock`, replacing `XXXXXXXX` with your unlock key.
11. `fastboot flash boot <path_to_patched_boot_image_on_computer>`
12. Boot the headset with `fastboot reboot`. The Magisk app should now show that Magisk is installed.
</Steps>

Your headset should now be rooted, and you are free to install Magisk modules. It is recommended that you go through Magisk's settings and enable Zygisk.
It is also recommended that you continue on to the next section.

## Restoring PICO 4 ABL

A necessary part of the unlocking process is to flash a PICO Neo3 ABL to your device, which has fastboot enabled.
However, this tends to result in your boot process being slow and unreliable (it is common for the headset to drop
into EDL mode, requiring a 15-second power button hold to recover from).

Once you have Magisk successfully installed, you may restore the PICO 4 ABL originally shipped with your device to fix
this. Your bootloader will remain unlocked, but fastboot commands will no longer function.

Requirements:
- Make sure you first follow [the Magisk installation.](#installing-magisk)
- Make sure you have adb installed, or use the one included with `picounlock`.

Steps:
<Steps>
1. Go the `picounlock` directory, then to `device-backup` and copy the `abl.bin` to the root of your PICO.
	* If you manually unlocked your headset or otherwise don't have a backed up ABL, then you can obtain one from
	an [OTA update](/guides/ota), within the `firmware-update` folder.
2. Be ready to put on your headset, after you run the commands below a prompt will appear that you will have to accept.
3. Then type `adb shell`, `su`.
(If you missed this prompt, open the Magisk app > Superuser > Enable shell)
4. You should now have a root shell.
5. Backup the Neo3 ABL for convenience:
`dd if=/dev/block-by-name/abl-picounlock.bin`
6. Now we will flash back the original (PICO 4) ABL:
`dd if=/sdcard/abl.bin of=/dev/block/by-name/abl`
7. Reboot your headset
</Steps>

Your device will now boot faster again, and you will still be able to use Magisk modules.

Should you need access to fashboot in future, you can temporarily reflash `abl-picounlock.bin` using `dd`, like step 6.
