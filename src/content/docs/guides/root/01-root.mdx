## Unlocking the bootloader

### Using `picounlock`
`picounlock` is an all-in-one tool for unlocking your PICO 4's bootloader.

[Download `picounlock` here (Windows, x86_64)](/picounlock/picounlock-windows-x86_64-v1.0.5_596340.zip)

<Steps>
1. Connect your headset to your computer via a reliable USB-C cable.
2. Download and run `picounlock` for your operating system.
3. Follow the steps in the GUI. If all goes well, your headset's bootloader will be unlocked!
4. Take note of the unlock key, displayed in the UI and printed in the log file. It looks like `picoXXXXXXXX`.
</Steps>

#### Putting back the original ABL.
Since this tool does (not yet...) flash back the original PICO 4 ABL, your device will be slow to boot, this is normal.  
We will therefore have to reflash the original ABL that was on your device.

Requirements:
- Make sure you first follow [the Magisk installation.](#installing-magisk)
- Make sure you have adb installed, or use the one included with `picounlock`.

Steps:
<Steps>
1. Go the `picounlock` directory, then to `device-backup` and copy the `abl.bin` to the root of your PICO.
2. Be ready to put on your headset, after you run the commands below a prompt will appear that you will have to accept.
3. Then type `adb shell`, `su`.  
(If you missed this prompt, open the Magisk app > Superuser > Enable shell)
4. You should now have a root shell.
5. Now we will flash back the original ABL:  
`dd if=/sdcard/abl.bin of=/dev/block/by-name/abl`
6. Type `reboot`.  
Your device will now boot faster again, and you will be able to use Magisk modules.
</Steps>

## Installing Magisk
<Steps>
1. You will need a copy of `adb`. If it is not installed on your computer, it is included with `picounlock`.
2. Download a [stock OTA image](/guides/ota) for your firmware version, and extract the `boot.img` to your computer.
3. [Download `Magisk4Pico.apk`](/magisk4pico/Magisk4Pico.apk) (stock Magisk will NOT work!).
4. `adb push boot.img /sdcard/Download`
5. `adb install Magisk4Pico.apk`
6. Open the `Magisk4Pico` app on your headset, and choose Install > Select and Patch a File, and choose `boot.img`.
7. In the output log, you will see the path to the freshly-patched boot image.
8. `adb pull <path_to_patched_boot_image_on_device>`
9. `adb reboot bootloader`
10. `fastboot oem picoXXXXXXXX unlock`, replacing `XXXXXXXX` with your unlock key.
11. `fastboot flash boot <path_to_patched_boot_image_on_computer>`
12. Boot the headset with `fastboot reboot`. The `Magisk4Pico` app should now show that Magisk is installed.
</Steps>

Your headset should now be rooted, and you are free to install Magisk modules. It is recommended that you go through Magisk's settings and enable Zygisk.

If you experience a black screen when installing modules, follow the [restoring your ABL guide](#putting-back-the-original-abl)
