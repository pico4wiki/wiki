<Aside type="caution">
	These instructions only cover `phoenix` headsets (PICO 4, PICO 4 Pro and PICO 4 Enterprise). They will not work on `sparrow` headsets (PICO 4 Ultra or Ultra Enterprise)!
</Aside>

<Aside type="danger">
	Rooting your headset and unlocking the bootloader **requires** your headset to be factory reset! **You will lose anything you do not back up!**
</Aside>

This tool can root your PICO 4 from a web browser, without installing any additional software.

You can find instructions on performing the process manually or with PicoUnlock for Windows on [the next page](/guides/root/01-root).
