## Before you begin

<Steps>
1. Download the correct firmware for your headset using [crx's guide](//pico.crx.moe/docs/picoos-research/version-table).
2. Connect your headset to your computer via a reliable USB-C cable.
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
You cannot downgrade to a firmware version lower than your current version, or switch your current firmware's region using OTAs.
</Aside>

## From PICO OS
<Steps>
1. Create a folder on the root of the headset's storage called `dload`.
2. Copy the firmware you downloaded into this folder without extracting it.
3. On the headset, go to Settings > General > System Update, and select Offline Update.
</Steps>

## From recovery mode
<Steps>
1. You will need a copy of `adb`. If it is not installed on your computer, it is included with `picounlock`.
1. On the headset, "Apply update from ADB".
2. `adb sideload <path_to_downloaded_firmware>`
3. Wait for it to finish (it will likely take some time).
4. Optionally, on the headset, "Wipe data/factory reset".
</Steps>
