---
title: Face Tracking with Baballonia
description: Using Baballonia to run eye and face tracking instead of onboard tracking
---

The PICO 4 Pro and PICO 4 Enterprise headsets feature on-headset eye tracking and face tracking.
The tracking from this is transmitted through PICO Connect and
[can be used with VRCFaceTracking](https://docs.vrcft.io/docs/hardware/vr/pico/pico4pe).

When these headsets are [rooted](/guides/root/01-root), you may instead send the raw camera feeds over the network to
[Baballonia](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4091970/Project_Babble_Baballonia/) to run the tracking on your PC.

This works well for eye tracking but less consistently for lower face tracking, due to the unconventional position of
the camera on these headsets.

## Steps

1. Root your headset following the relevant guide. Make sure you enable Zygisk.
2. Enable eye tracking and face tracking in settings
3. Calibrate eye tracking now - while eyed is enabled, calibration does not work.
4. Download [eyed](/eyed/eyed-magisk-module-1.0.zip) and [eyestream](/eyestream/eyestream-magisk-module-1.0.zip) on the headset, place them into the `Download` folder,
   and install them from Magisk. (TODO: download links)
5. Reboot your headset
6. Open Baballonia, find your headset's IP address, and set the camera feeds as:
    - http://(headset IP):4442/left
    - http://(headset IP):4442/right
    - http://(headset IP):4442/mouth
