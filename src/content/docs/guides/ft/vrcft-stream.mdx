---
title: VRCFT without PICO Connect
description: Using onboard tracking and sending it to VRCFT without using PICO Connect
---

The PICO 4 Pro and PICO 4 Enterprise headsets feature on-headset eye tracking and face tracking.
The tracking from this is transmitted through PICO Connect and
[can be used with VRCFaceTracking](//docs.vrcft.io/docs/hardware/vr/pico/pico4pe).

However, if you are using Steam Link, ALVR, or Virtual Desktop, you cannot use this method.

When these headsets are [rooted](/guides/root/01-root), you may instead send the tracking data from the PICO XR API to
your computer over a side channel and have VRCFaceTracking use that.

This also gives slightly improved tracking quality than PICO Connect as it is using a newer version of the face tracking
protocol, and is in my experience more stable, but can have issues with jittery tracking - YMMV.

You should not combine this method with [eyed](/guides/ft/baballonia).

## Steps

1. Root your headset following the relevant guide. Make sure you enable Zygisk.
2. Enable `Eye Tracking` and `Face Tracking` in `Settings > LAB`.
3. Download the `picofacialdatadaemon-magisk-module.zip` from [thoricelli/PicoFacialDataDaemon](//github.com/thoricelli/PicoFacialDataDaemon/releases)
   on your headset, place it in the `Download` folder, and flash it via Magisk.
4. Reboot your headset.
5. Download the `picofacialdatamodule.zip` from [thoricelli/PicoFacialDataModule](//github.com/thoricelli/PicoFacialDataModule/releases)
   on your computer, open VRCFaceTracking, and go to `Module Registry`, click the plus at the top, and select the zip file.
