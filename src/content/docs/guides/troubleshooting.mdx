## How do I get into the bootloader/recovery?

While booting your headset, hold volume down to access the bootloader, or hold volume up to access recovery.

While in recovery, you will see an Android symbol and "No command" text. To get past this screen to the actual recovery
interface, hold down power, press volume up, then release power.

If you are booted into PICO OS, you can use ADB to reboot to recovery (`adb reboot recovery` / `adb reboot bootloader`).

You can access Qualcomm EDL mode from PICO OS or recovery with `adb reboot edl`.
It cannot be reached from the bootloader or a powered off state, but the headset will enter EDL if a boot fails.

## My headset won't charge or turn on

Your headset may be in Qualcomm EDL mode. In this mode, the screen is blank, the battery will not charge,
holding the power button for a few seconds will not do anything, and the headset will identify itself as a
Qualcomm modem over USB instead of the usual old Nokia phone that a powered-off PICO 4 reports (???).

To get out of this state, hold the power button for 15 seconds.

## Which USB device is my headset?

A PICO 4 Enterprise reports over USB as:
- `Pico PICO 4 Enterprise` (PICO OS, Recovery mode)
- `Google Inc. Xiaomi Mi/Redmi 2 (fastboot)` (Bootloader)
- `Qualcomm, Inc. Gobi Wireless Modem (QDL mode)` (Qualcomm EDL mode)
- `Qualcomm, Inc. TA-1004 [Nokia 8]` (Powered off)

If anyone has a PICO 4 or PICO 4 Pro that reports differently, PR it!

## PICO Connect has an unacceptable microphone latency

Some users find that their microphone slowly builds up an unacceptable latency (multiple seconds) with PICO Connect.

This is caused by the PICO streaming microphone going to sleep when not in use, and then picking up more latency when
it wakes up again. Hence, it can be worked around by setting up a virtual audio device to keep it active at all times.

<Steps>
	1. Install [VB-CABLE](https://vb-audio.com/Cable) (extract it and run VBCABLE_Setup_x64)
	2. Right click your volume mixer > Sound Settings
	3. Scroll down and open More sound settings, and go to the Recording tab
	4. Select PicoStreamingMicrophone and open the Properties
	5. In the Listen tab, enable *Listen to this Device* and set *Playback through this device* to CABLE Input
</Steps>

Now Windows will route the audio from the PICO Connect mic into VB-CABLE at all times, and works around the latency issue.

If you still experience latency issues, try disabling Headset Microphone Noise Reduction in PICO Connect.
This should not be necessary most of the time, though.
